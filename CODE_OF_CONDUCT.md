# Code of Conduct

ERD Go contributors are expected to make participation welcoming, respectful, and constructive.

- Be considerate, patient, and specific when discussing code or design.
- Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, or disclosure of private information.
- Respect maintainers’ decisions about scope, safety, and project direction.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options on the GitHub repository. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
